🤸 Flip and Flan - Bouncing Game!

Two Player Turn-Based Bouncing Game!
🧒 James and 👨 Daddy take turns bouncing on trampolines!
When one player lands safely, it's the other player's turn!

Controls:
⬅️ Left Arrow or A = Move Left
➡️ Right Arrow or D = Move Right
🚀 Space Bar = Extra Bounce (when on trampoline)
Collect ⭐ stars while bouncing to score points!
Do flips and tricks to rack up your flip count!
Bounce on different colored trampolines - some bounce higher than others! 🎪
