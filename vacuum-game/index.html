How to Play

Use the arrow keys to move James the vacuum around the room. Vacuum up all the houses 🏠, people 👥, and Lego blocks 🧱 to win! But be careful - don't crash into the red warning signs ⚠️ or you'll lose! When the vacuum gets full (10 items), it will beep and you can press SPACEBAR or click the button to return to the charger 🔌 to empty it.
