🏁 Fun Obstacles - Round Race Track 🏁

Jason's Turn | Jason: 0 | James: 0 | Battery: 100%

🏎️ Controls

Two Player Turn-Based Game!
🚗 Jason (Red Car) and 🚕 James (Yellow Car) take turns!
When one player's car breaks down (hits obstacle/runs out of battery), it's the other player's turn!

Controls:
🚀 Space Bar or Up Arrow = Go Up
⬇️ Down Arrow = Go Down
⬅️ Left Arrow = Go Left
➡️ Right Arrow = Go Right
Collect 🔋 batteries to keep your car running! Avoid obstacles and other cars!
Visit the Hospital 🏥, School 🏫, Playground 🛝, and Pak N Save 🛒!
